# Jot Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

Jot is a notes app for Mac. It is built to keep your notes on your Mac and nowhere else.

## What Jot collects

Nothing. Jot doesn't collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, usage data, diagnostics, or identifiers. It contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking code, and it doesn't create accounts.

## Where your notes are stored

Your notes, and Jot's settings such as the window position and keyboard shortcut, are saved only on your Mac, inside Jot's own storage. Jot never uploads them. If you back up your Mac (for example with Time Machine), your notes are included in that backup under your control.

## Files you choose

Jot reads a file only when you pick it in the Import panel, and it writes a file only where you choose in the Export panel.

## Links

When you click a web link in a note, Jot opens it in your default browser. Your browser's own privacy policy applies to that website.

## Children

Jot doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
